Why the museum?

Because heritage preserved is a future built

Ethiopia has a rich and diverse heritage of languages, manuscripts, scholarship, and historical knowledge. Preserving it helps future generations understand their history — and lets researchers and wider audiences engage with these resources.

✦

Preservation

Document and protect manuscripts, historical records, and cultural knowledge before they are lost.

❧

Research

Support the study of Ethiopian history, languages, philosophy, astronomy, and numerical traditions.

▣

Education

Build learning programs and resources for students, educators, families, and visitors.

◎

Digital access

Use responsible digital technology — archives and online resources — to open heritage to the world.
